# Steward

**An AI agent that's on your side when a company isn't, and that belongs to you.**

Steward cancels subscriptions, disputes charges, chases refunds and follows up on complaints. It keeps going until the money is back, and it asks before it spends or sends anything. It's for anyone with an iPhone and a pile of life admin, with no setup and no API keys, in any country.

> Steward is a working name. The project is early: an iPhone app shell exists, and the agent does not yet.

## Focus

We focus on the errands where a company is on the other side: **cancel, dispute, refund, complain.** They're done in writing, they follow written rules, and success is clear: either the money came back or it didn't.

Steward works in every country from day one. Where tested playbooks exist, it uses them and shows their success rate. Where they don't, it tells you before you start.

## Our advantage

The agent's building blocks are becoming standard parts, and the best AI models keep improving. We use both rather than compete with them. What we aim to own:

- **Proof.** Every errand is a case that stays open until the outcome is confirmed. Every playbook has a public, measured success rate.
- **Paid only by you.** No ads, no referral fees, no paid placement.
- **Yours to keep.** Your data is readable, exportable, deletable and works with any AI. No company, including us, has to be trusted with it.
- **Nothing hidden.** You approve every payment, message and irreversible step. Every action is logged, and all the code is open.

## How we grow

Finished errands produce confirmed outcomes. Those make playbooks better, and public results earn the trust for the next errand. Contributors anywhere add playbooks for the companies and countries they know.

## V1

1. **Test first.** Run the four errands in several countries three ways: a top model alone, a cheap model with a playbook, and an existing consumer agent. If the cheap model with a playbook doesn't win, we stop or change the plan.
2. **Then ship one errand end to end.** You get chat, a case view, email you approve before it sends, follow-ups over weeks, a rule-based approval gate, an activity log and full export.

**Done when** ten non-technical strangers across several countries finish a real errand without help, with nothing sent, spent or shared that they didn't approve.

## V2

All four errands, with escalation to regulators and chargebacks. Public success rates for each playbook, opt-in outcome sharing with no personal details, any AI model or your own key, a browser only as a last resort, and tools for contributors.
